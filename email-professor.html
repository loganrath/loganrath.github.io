Before you send: quick etiquette checklist

  • Check the syllabus and course site first — your question may already be answered there.
  • Use a professional greeting: "Dear Professor Last-Name," and the honorific they use (Professor or Dr.), not "Hey" or first names unless invited to.
  • Use your official school email address and a clear subject line that includes your course name/number.
  • Keep it concise: state who you are, why you're writing, and what you'd like them to do.
  • Proofread before sending, and always end with a thank you.
  • If you don't hear back in about a week, a brief, polite follow-up is okay.
